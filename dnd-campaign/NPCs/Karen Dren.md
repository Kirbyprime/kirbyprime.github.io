## Profile
%%
An imposing Paladin of Devotion, also known as Karen the Relentless. A fanatic in every sense of the word. Karen believes that all should be judged by the sword and none should be spared. Not even herself.

Karen is blind and missing her right hand. Both were self imposed. They were ritualistically removed to cleanse her sins and bring herself closer to her Patron god [[Tyr]], the deity of justice. Although she should not be able to see, it does not seem to hinder her in any way. Her devotion to Tyr seemingly giving her a sixth sense to compensate for her lack of sight.

## Possessions
%%
Karen carries a standard issue military Greatsword and Longbow, believing that skill should be prioritized over exotic or enchanted weaponry. Even without such enchantments, her devotion to [[Tyr]] allows her to smite enemies with divine wrath making any weapon she wields equally deadly.
