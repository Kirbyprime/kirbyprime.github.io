## Profile  

Once a daughter of House Vandree. Vierna's name was stripped from the family records when she dared talk of honour and respect to the Matron Mother. The Matron, enraged by her un-Drow beliefs sold her to surface slavers so that she can be among the primitives that she apparently respected.

It was not until 8 years later when she would be rescued and freed by [[Karen Dren]] who was traveling through Fairun, spreading the tenants of Tyr and that of law and order. Vierna was greatly moved by one who embodied her past ideals and vowed to follow her, soon becoming her Squire.
