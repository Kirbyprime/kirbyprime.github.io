## Tactics
The Inquisition comes in from the front of the settlement in full display. 

If the party fought [[Bone Naga]], then they will ambush the [[The Party|party]] as a transition to the next scenario.

If the party hides, or otherwise attempts to leave from the back. They would need to escape from an entrapment.

> The party is exhausted and battered. XXXX readies him/herself to delivery the final critical blow towards the undead serpent. Before you attack though, you catch sight of what appears to be a fully armoured knight. The knight calmly but briskly walks up to serpent and with practiced ease, slashes it three times with their great sword. 
 
Roll 3 times: 1d20 +9 to hit for (2d6 + 5) slashing damage and (5d8) radiant damage.

After the serpent is dead, have the entire party choose to do a CON, DEX, or WIS savings throw. Remind them to check their skills and abilities for anything that affects saving throws.
- **9 or lower:** A sudden blow from behind, and everything goes black.
- **10-14:** You glimpse your attacker and the emblem they wear. _(History or Religion DC 10: the Order of Tyr.)_
- **15-17:** Another knight approaches the one who killed the serpent. "Madam Dren, our forces have finished scouting the area. We captured two cultists just outside town and sent them back for questioning."
- **18+:** A beautiful Drow approaches and hands the Knight a scroll with a wax seal. The Knight opens it, pauses, then crushes it in her fist. "Take your prisoners," she says coldly, and leaves. The Drow turns to the other knight. "My, my. Karen is such a prude, don't you think?" Without waiting for an answer, she continues, "Now run along. These fine specimens are mine..."
