Using the Attack action, you can make a special melee attack to shove a creature, either to knock it prone or push it away from you. The target must succeed on a ==🔴Strength or Dexterity saving throw== (it chooses which), or you either ==🔴push it 5 feet away or cause it to have the Prone condition==. The ==🔴DC for the saving throw equals 8 plus your Strength modifier and Proficiency Bonus==. This shove is possible only if the ==🔴target is no more than one size larger== than you.
